# Site redesign direction contract

## THESIS

SeatMate is a night route atlas where drivers' available seats and passengers' destinations meet. The site shows the trip mechanism itself, not a generic transport landing page.

## OWN-WORLD

Deep asphalt, smoked graphite surfaces, warm white text, and restrained signal red. Road photography, fine route lines, compact wayfinding labels, and a clean editorial sans voice replace pixel lettering and glowing neon decoration.

## STORY

Visitors immediately understand that SeatMate is for shared trips across Belarus. Passengers can search a route; drivers can publish one. The same route, departure, price, seat, and driver details stay legible through the trip, booking, chat, and profile screens.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At desktop, a shallow floating header frames a full-width night-road image. A large left-aligned headline names shared travel; a compact animated route line leads into the real search controls. A secondary driver action remains visible beside the passenger search. On mobile, the image becomes a cropped backdrop above a stacked, thumb-friendly search panel.

## FORM

Code-led route atlas, selected by the assistant after the user delegated visual choices and confirmed balanced passenger/driver priority. Preserve the existing site structure and functions while replacing its pixel-arcade look with road-oriented editorial wayfinding.
